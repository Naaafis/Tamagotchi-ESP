# Quick start (for Christina)

## Turn it on

Plug the power bank into the board's USB cable. After a second or two the screen lights up, you hear a short two-note hello, and an egg appears. It wobbles, cracks and hatches (about 6 seconds) into one of five critters, picked at random: Sprout (a leaf on top), Dabble (big duck lips), Ribbon (a big bow), Heartlet (a heart on top) or Lyra (bunny ears). The buttons do nothing until it's hatched.

## Buttons

| Button | Press | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| **Left** | move to the next icon | 3 s: make it poop now (for showing people) |
| **Middle** | select the icon | 10 s: a new egg, which always hatches a different critter (works anytime, even after it's gone) |
| **Right** | back / close | 1 s in the game: quit the game. 10 s: sound off (a falling "bye"), or back on (the hello) |

Any button wakes it up while it's asleep.

## Icons

Top row:

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Burger | **Eat.** It takes three bites. If it's full it shakes its head and won't eat. |
| Cup | **Drink.** Same as eating: three sips, and it says no when it's full. |
| Moon | **Sleep.** It yawns and lies down, the lights go out and Z's float up. It wakes by itself when rested, or press any button. |
| Gamepad | **Play** the left/right game, 5 rounds. Arrows show on both sides: guess which way it will turn, **left** = left, **right** = right. 3 or more right out of 5 is a win and makes it very happy. |

Bottom row:

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Duck | **Clean.** A wave sweeps across and washes the poops away. |
| Heart | **Affection.** Hearts pop up around it. |
| Bars | **Stats.** How full each need is, plus how clean it is. Right or middle closes it. |
| ! (bottom right, not selectable) | Blinks and chirps when it needs something. The small icon next to the pet shows what it wants. |

## Looking after it

- It asks for something about every 10 minutes, and poops every 6-10 minutes (sooner after eating or drinking). Up to 4 poops pile up next to it; while it's dirty it gets sad faster.
- When a need runs out, it turns away and sulks. Give it what it wants and it cheers up.
- If nobody presses a button for 15 minutes, or it's left sulking for about 10 minutes, a UFO comes and takes it home. Any button press restarts the 15 minutes (it doesn't count while it's asleep). Hold **middle for 10 seconds** for a new pet.

## If something goes wrong

- **It restarts by itself** (screen flickers, hello sound, a new egg): a wire got jostled or the game hit a bug, and it started itself over within a few seconds. Nothing to do, but if it keeps happening, check the jumpers and tell Nafis.
- **Screen blank or frozen, or the buttons do nothing:** unplug the power bank, wait 2 seconds, plug it back in. The pet starts fresh.
- **No sound:** it may have been turned off: hold right for 10 seconds until the hello plays (unplugging and replugging also turns it back on). Still nothing? Check the speaker wires at the amp. It works fine without sound.
