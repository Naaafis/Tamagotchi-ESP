# Quick start (for Christina)

## Turn it on

Plug the power bank into the board's USB cable. After a second or two the screen lights up, you hear a short two-note hello, and Sprout (the little critter with the leaf) appears.

## Buttons

| Button | Press | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| **Left** | move to the next icon | 3 s: make it poop now (for showing people) |
| **Middle** | select the icon | 10 s: start a new pet (works anytime, even after it's gone) |
| **Right** | back / close | 1 s in the game: quit the game |

Any button wakes it up while it's asleep.

## Icons

Top row:

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Burger | **Eat.** It takes three bites. If it's full it shakes its head and won't eat. |
| Cup | **Drink.** Same as eating: three sips, and it says no when it's full. |
| Moon | **Sleep.** It yawns and lies down, the lights go out and Z's float up. It wakes by itself when rested, or press any button. |
| Gamepad | **Play** the left/right game, 5 rounds. Arrows show on both sides: guess which way it will turn, **left** = left, **right** = right. 3 or more right out of 5 is a win and makes it very happy. |

Bottom row:

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Duck | **Clean.** A wave sweeps across and washes the poops away. |
| Heart | **Affection.** Hearts pop up around it. |
| Bars | **Stats.** How full each need is, plus how clean it is. Right or middle closes it. |
| ! (bottom right, not selectable) | Blinks and chirps when it needs something. The small icon next to the pet shows what it wants. |

## Looking after it

- It asks for something about every 10 minutes, and poops every 6-10 minutes (sooner after eating or drinking). Up to 4 poops pile up next to it; while it's dirty it gets sad faster.
- When a need runs out, it turns away and sulks. Give it what it wants and it cheers up.
- If it's left sulking for about 10 minutes, a UFO comes and takes it home. Hold **middle for 10 seconds** for a new pet.

## If something goes wrong

- **Screen blank or frozen, or the buttons do nothing:** unplug the power bank, wait 2 seconds, plug it back in. The pet starts fresh.
- **No sound:** check the speaker wires at the amp. It still works without sound.
